WHAT CHANGED
- Day-level on-call allocations with weekly inheritance, alternate technician
  and an explicit open-day option, while approved leave remains blocked.
- Six-month planning across 26 Monday-start weeks grouped by month.
- Quiet confirmation for roster, day cover and order changes via the Email staff
  checkbox; changes are still recorded in Recent activity.
- Numbered rotation-position selector replacing the previous arrow controls.
- Day-cover and six-month dialogs fit 834x480 and 390x844. Only their internal
  day/week lists scroll, and background scrolling is locked while open. The
  current-week actions panel is kept wide enough for all four actions, and
  day-cover chips no longer lift, flicker or underline on hover.
- Book appointment type dropdown supports Other / custom type.
- Icon-only Quick client entry copy button is centred on desktop and mobile.
- Schedule block checkbox is borderless, top-left and centred in the green rail.
- Stock expand/collapse uses the shared Feather chevron; stock flow count
  contrast is fixed, active flow buttons stay dark, and icons stay inside buttons.
- Schedule sticky bar contains only Save schedule settings; Add appointment type
  is in its card heading. Settings save/reload bars remain visible while scrolling.
- Leave requests use a sticky right-side summary card on desktop, stacking below
  the form on narrow screens. Breakdowns work for one day or a partial hour, and
  the primary action is Submit.
- Email quote restores the Outlook .eml download with recipient, configured
  subject/body and generated PDF attachment.
