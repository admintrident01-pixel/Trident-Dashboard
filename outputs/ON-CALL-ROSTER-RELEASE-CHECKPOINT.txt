SCOPE
- Adds an On call page showing the current technician, next technician and a
  six-week roster.
- Technicians can accept, hand over, release to the public pool or grab an open
  week. First successful grab wins.
- Admins can edit the weekly rotation order, assign an open week and resend the
  acceptance email.
- Every roster change queues email to all active verified technicians/admins;
  local development records previews only and sends no real email.
- Approved leave blocks a technician from receiving or grabbing that week.
