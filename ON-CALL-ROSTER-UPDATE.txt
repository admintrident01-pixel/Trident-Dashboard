Weekly on-call roster, 13 November 2026 (Pacific/Auckland)

SCOPE
- New On call page in the main navigation for technicians and admins.
- Shows who is on call this week, who is next, a six-week roster and the
  admin-defined rotation order.
- Technicians accept their own week, hand it to another technician, or release
  it to the open pool. An approved-leave technician cannot receive or grab a
  week they are away.
- Any technician can grab an open week; the first successful grab wins.
- Admins edit the rotation order with up/down controls and can assign an open
  week back to a technician.
- The assigned technician or an admin can resend the acceptance email.
- Every change queues an email to all active, verified technicians and admins.
  Local development keeps those messages as previews and never sends them.

WEEK RULES
- Weeks run Monday 12:00 am to Sunday 11:59 pm Pacific/Auckland.
- Current and next week are published as Awaiting accept. The next week's
  deadline is Sunday 5 pm; if it is missed, the week joins the open pool.
- Future weeks remain Scheduled until they become current/next.
- The current-week card confirms availability without showing a past deadline.
