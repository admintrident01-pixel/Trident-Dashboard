Time sheet validation, draft deletion and leave detail popup - 17 November 2026

Direct-hours entries now carry the normal 07:30-16:00 start and finish when the
default eight-hour day is used. Entering custom hours requires matching actual
start and finish times, and a mismatch blocks submission with the discrepancy
shown in the form.

Draft handling now lets admins delete every draft while technicians can delete
only their own. Submitted time sheets and historical records remain unchanged.

Leave history separates the leave type, mixed-type detail and Other description,
and shows the total hours separately from Paid and Unpaid amounts. Selecting a
request opens its details in a modal with close, Escape and backdrop dismissal.

Checks passed: ESLint, production Vite build, 192 frontend tests and 89 focused
backend tests covering direct hours, weekly Excel defaults, discrepancies, draft
permissions, leave breakdowns, payables descriptions and concurrency. No stored
data schema or business record was changed by this update.
