Lunch checkbox clarity — 12 October 2026

New time sheets keep unpaid lunch checked by default. A dedicated Unpaid lunch column sits next to Payable hours. Checked says 30 min unpaid and deducts 0.5 hours; unchecked says No deduction and includes those 30 minutes in payable hours. 07:30-16:00 gives 8 paid hours checked or 8.5 unchecked. Hours before lunch clearly labels the gross input. Existing submitted sheets retain their saved lunch choice. Admin correction popup uses the same clearer wording.

Verified in both entry methods with tests, and in the browser: Monday unchecked changed 8.00 to 8.50 and weekly 40.00 to 40.50. Rechecking restored 40.00. No saved submission was changed. Frontend lint/build passed.
