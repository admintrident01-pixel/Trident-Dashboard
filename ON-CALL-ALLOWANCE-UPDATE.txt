On-call allowance update

The time sheet sign-off now asks a compulsory On-call allowance question next to
"Signed by", and the answer follows the week through to the payables reports.

Time sheet form
- New amber Yes / No control directly under "Signed by", marked Required so it is
  visually separate from the plain fields around it.
- Signing, submitting or generating the PDF is blocked until Yes or No is chosen.
  The message reads "Choose Yes or No for the on-call allowance."
- Choosing Yes shows "Weekly notes, labour payables and the weekly Excel export
  will show On-call allowance needed."
- Saving a draft keeps whatever was chosen, and a draft may still be saved with
  the question unanswered. Reopening a draft restores the answer.
- The downloaded time sheet PDF prints "On-call allowance: Yes" (or No) and adds
  "On-call allowance needed" as the first weekly note when Yes is chosen.

Labour payables
- Each person row carries the answer for their week. When Yes, the weekly Total
  column shows an amber "On-call allowance needed" badge under the hours.
- Admin corrections show the same Yes / No field in the time sheet editor, and the
  original answer is kept when an older sheet is corrected.

Weekly Excel export
- The Notes column on each person's "Weekly total" row reads
  "On-call allowance needed" when that week was marked Yes.

Records
- The choice is stored as onCall (true / false) on the submitted sheet and on the
  draft. It is never copied into the typed weekly or daily notes, so the text is
  not duplicated if the week is edited or re-exported.
- Existing sheets without onCall stay readable. They are treated as "no on-call
  allowance recorded", and an admin may set the answer the next time a correction
  is saved.

Also in this working tree
- Leave form: the "Different lunch time" option is removed. Part-day leave now
  offers only "Usual lunch" or "No unpaid lunch applies" (with a reason when there
  is no unpaid lunch).
