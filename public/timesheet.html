How hours and lunch work

Enter direct hours or start and finish times in 15-minute increments. Choose work or leave for each day and add daily notes. Lunch defaults to Unpaid (30 minutes). Choose Paid — lunch not taken to include that half hour. Enter hours before lunch, or use Start / Finish. 7:30 am–4:00 pm pays 8 hours with Unpaid lunch, or 8.5 hours with Paid. Use the time arrows, keyboard arrows or scroll inside a time field to change it by 15 minutes. Totals update before you sign.
