Card expansion lab

Live CSS variables. Hover the William Mane sample or a leave block to test the grow animation.

Delay holds the card closed until the pointer settles, then the card grows downward just enough to fit its own content. The max height is only a safety cap; a card never shrinks below its scheduled block height. The edge zoom is the existing 2px surface/rail growth. Scale stays at 1 by default so text remains sharp; raise it for a subtle zoom.

Try the interaction states: hover or keyboard-focus any block except travel and it grows in place just enough to fit its own text — no browser tooltip or floating detail panel. Bookings also get the quick 2px surface zoom, the diagonal light sweep and their status rail; leave, lunch and note blocks keep their flat own tint but expand too. Expanded cards show the total duration once, not the start and finish again. Travel blocks never move. These are mock examples only; no appointment, travel, leave or note record is created.
